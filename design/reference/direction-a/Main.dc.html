Why one set

I'd retire Public Sans and Red Hat Mono in the console. Mukta was drawn for Devanagari and Latin together, so a Hindi guest name or a "खम्मा घणी" greeting sets cleanly beside English — Public Sans has no Devanagari. One mono for numbers means a bill reads the same on the guest's phone and the desk's printer.

04

The jharokha

214
Full cusped arch: room home hero and the printed QR sticker. Nowhere else. Simplified arch: a frame for the room number, empty states. Console: the outline only, as the product mark and loading glyph.
05

Icons & marks

Lucide line icons at 1.5px stroke, 20 or 24px. Veg and non-veg use the FSSAI square marks Indian guests already read — dot for veg, triangle for non-veg — so shape carries meaning, not just green and brown. Spice is three small chilli strokes, filled count = heat, with "Mild / Medium / Hot" in text beside.
