01 · NO QR TO MANAGE
One sticker per room. Set once, never touched again.

Most hotel QR systems make you generate a code per guest, print it, and hope housekeeping swaps it. Ours is fixed to the room. The stay does the rest.

A
The code is the room, not the guest. When your PMS checks Anjali in, the sticker starts resolving to her stay. No printing, no pairing.
B
Nothing to install, nothing to log in to. The phone camera opens a page that already knows the room. The first charge asks for one verification.
C
At checkout the code stops working. The next guest cannot see the last one's folio. A photo of the sticker is worthless after 11am.
34 stickers for a 34-room hotel. Printed on day one. Still the same stickers in year three.
02 · PRICES THAT MOVE WITH YOUR NIGHT
Charge for late checkout when the room is wanted. Give it away when it isn't.

A fixed late checkout fee is wrong twice a day: too high on a Tuesday with nine empty rooms, too low on a Saturday when the next guest is waiting in the lobby. Innstant reads tomorrow's arrivals and prices the extra hours accordingly.
